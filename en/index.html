🤖 Riding with an Android?

The Android version exists: it's in closed testing on Google Play while we polish it with a small group of cyclists. You're welcome to join: leave me your address and I'll add you to the list.

Be sure to give the address of the Google account you use on your phone: that address, and only that one, unlocks access to the test. You'll then get a link to join the test; once you're in, CycloApp installs from the Play Store like any other app, and updates itself.
